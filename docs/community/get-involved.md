# Get involved

## Join the FirstCut movement

There are several ways to contribute to the portfolio.

---

## For users
- explore the apps that matter to you
- provide feedback on what works and what feels missing
- share ideas that improve real-world outcomes

## For builders
- contribute to product design and development
- help improve the user experience across the ecosystem
- support the roadmap and feature priorities

## For partners
- explore collaboration opportunities
- share insight into customer needs and ecosystem fit
- support awareness and market growth

## For investors
- review the portfolio roadmap and opportunity
- assess the ecosystem model and multi-app strategic logic
- support structured growth and expansion

---

## Simple next step

Pick your lane and start by exploring the relevant app or strategy page.
