# Roadmap

## FirstCut portfolio roadmap

The FirstCut ecosystem is being built in stages so that traction, trust and value grow in a deliberate sequence.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation and visibility

### Goals
- create a clear portfolio narrative
- align all five apps under one strategic mission
- improve live and deployed experiences
- define core app scopes and dependencies

### Priority work
- MintWall growth and onboarding refinement
- CarTune UI and functionality upgrades
- DFTC product and storefront planning
- LetLive persona and MVP definition
- OffIce concept validation and workflow mapping

---

## Phase 2 — Revenue and traction

### Goals
- build the first monetisation and commercial layer
- improve conversion and retention
- grow the user base across the ecosystem

### Priority work
- DFTC MVP launch
- MintWall funnel and communication strategy
- CarTune UX stabilisation and trust building
- LetLive validation in care and support flows

---

## Phase 3 — Social impact and real-world utility

### Goals
- deepen the community value of the portfolio
- create practical support pathways and automation for real-world users
- build the next layer of operational usefulness

### Priority work
- LetLive early community engagement and support design
- OffIce operational workflow MVP
- ecosystem cross-promotion and conversion strategy

---

## Phase 4 — Scale and extension

### Goals
- expand reach and monetisation
- deepen partner and community relationships
- improve the portfolio through real usage data

### Priority work
- growth loops and retention optimisation
- partnership development
- product maturity and scale management
- marketing and communications around the wider ecosystem

---

## Key principle

Each app should support the growth of the others.

The portfolio should compound value, not simply add more isolated products.

---

## Current focus

The highest-priority work in the near term is:

1. MintWall growth
2. CarTune usability and trust
3. DFTC commerce foundation
4. LetLive impact and support design
5. OffIce workflow validation

---

## Success indicators

- user growth
- retention and interaction quality
- conversion from audience to customer
- trust and product usability
- community value and real-world utility

---

Choose the app you want to build, improve or launch first, then connect it back to the wider FirstCut strategy.
