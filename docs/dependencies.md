# Cross-app dependencies

## How the apps fit together

The FirstCut portfolio is strongest when the apps reinforce one another. The dependency model is designed to create shared momentum rather than fragmented work.

---

## Core dependency map

### MintWall
- growth and audience foundation
- promotes the wider ecosystem
- provides trust-building entry points
- supports all other apps through user acquisition

### CarTune
- user trust layer
- improves product confidence
- informs experience design and user journey thinking
- strengthens the brand feel across the portfolio

### DFTC
- commerce engine and revenue layer
- leverages audience and product trust
- provides a customer-facing brand experience
- supports the wider portfolio with commercial sustainability

### LetLive
- community impact layer
- requires trust, empathy and clear user journeys
- benefits from user growth and audience clarity
- strengthens public purpose and partnership opportunities

### OffIce
- real-world operational utility layer
- benefits from streamlined business workflows
- provides practical value to working Australians
- creates professional relevance and function-led adoption

---

## Strategic relationships

- MintWall feeds CarTune, DFTC, LetLive and OffIce by generating users and interest
- CarTune improves trust for all downstream app experiences
- DFTC creates commercial viability and brand direction
- LetLive adds purpose and human-centred value
- OffIce gives the portfolio practical utility in the real world

---

## Portfolio principle

No app is being built as a silo.

Each one is connected to the broader portfolio narrative and launch plan.
