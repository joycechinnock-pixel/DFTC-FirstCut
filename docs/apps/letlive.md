# LetLive

## Status: Building

LetLive is the community and impact layer of the FirstCut ecosystem.

It is designed to support elderly Australians living at home, connect people to housing options, provide access to relevant support pathways, and create skill-building opportunities for both care workers and community members.

---

## What it does

LetLive is a purpose-led platform with a practical mission:

- keep elderly people supported in their homes
- help people find suitable housing pathways
- connect support networks and care opportunities
- make government funding information more accessible
- create upskilling pathways and support education

---

## Why it matters in the portfolio

This app is the human-impact layer.

It gives FirstCut a real social value proposition and strengthens the portfolio with community trust, public relevance and meaningful purpose.
