# Deployment guide

## Portfolio deployment approach

The FirstCut series should be deployed in phases and tracked clearly by app and by portfolio milestone.

---

## Recommended deployment model

### Phase 1
- public portfolio and mission visibility
- launch or refine the live apps
- maintain a clear board of priorities

### Phase 2
- release app-specific MVPs in order of readiness
- connect product updates to the portfolio roadmap
- track usage, conversion and trust signals

### Phase 3
- coordinate wider ecosystem rollout
- expand cross-app marketing and conversion loops
- ensure app pacing reflects readiness and real-world value delivery

---

## Operational rules

- progress should be tracked at the app level and portfolio level
- each app should have a clear definition of completion
- releases should follow readiness, not urgency alone
- deployment planning should consider user value and business value together

---

## Launch sequencing

The recommended order remains:

1. MintWall
2. CarTune
3. DFTC
4. LetLive
5. OffIce

This sequence creates a strong base before the more complex community and operational layers are expanded.
