# Contributing guide

## How to contribute

Contributions are welcome across product, design, research, community, business and technical work.

---

## Ways to help

### Product and strategy
- roadmap feedback
- feature prioritisation
- user journey analysis
- business model input

### Design and brand
- visual polish
- user interface suggestions
- product storytelling and positioning
- design systems and creative direction

### Technical work
- frontend or backend implementation
- product improvement tasks
- bug fixes and QA
- deployment and infrastructure support

### Community and growth
- user acquisition ideas
- partner relationships
- outreach and collaboration opportunities
- community engagement and awareness building

---

## Contribution workflow

1. pick an app or a strategic area
2. review the roadmap and app goals
3. propose the improvement or issue
4. track the work with the relevant project board or issue
5. contribute clearly and keep updates visible

---

## Good contribution principle

Always make sure the work improves the app or the portfolio without distracting from the mission.
