# Portfolio overview

## DFTC – For Our People

The FirstCut portfolio is a connected ecosystem of five products designed to bring real value to everyday Australians.

This is a portfolio strategy, not just a product strategy.

---

## The portfolio story

The portfolio begins with MintWall, builds user trust through CarTune, creates a revenue engine with DFTC, deepens purpose with LetLive, and adds practical business utility with OffIce.

The result is a system that can support growth, value and impact at the same time.

---

## What makes the model strong

- shared growth engine
- clearer mission alignment
- layered product rollout
- stronger stakeholder story
- wider market relevance

---

## Strategic view

This is not about building random apps. It is about building a connected portfolio that can scale over time while delivering genuine public value.
