# Partnership opportunities

## Build with FirstCut

The FirstCut ecosystem creates opportunities for partners in several spaces:

- product collaboration
- community support and outreach
- creative and brand partnerships
- growth and channel partnerships
- service and operations partnership

---

## Ideal partners

- community organisations
- local businesses and service networks
- impact-minded agencies
- creators and cultural brands
- operational service providers

---

## What partners can help with

- user acquisition and shared audience strategy
- service access and resource networks
- funding pathways and community support
- practical delivery and operations support
- product awareness and trust building

---

## Partnership principle

The strongest relationships will be the ones that help the portfolio create value for real people while staying aligned with the mission.
